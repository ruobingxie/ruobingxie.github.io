Bio

Ruobing Xie is a senior researcher of Tencent Hy. He received his BEng degree in 2014 and his master degree in 2017 from the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tsinghua University. His research interests include Large Language Model, Pre-training, and Recommender System. He has published 100+ papers in top-tier conferences and journals including ACL, ICML, NeurIPS, KDD, AAAI, with 17000+ citations according to Google Scholar. He has received the first prize of the Natural Science Award (Science and Technology) of the Ministry of Education, RecSys 2024 Best Full Paper, RecSys 2023 Best Short Paper, the Paper Award Nominations of TheWebConf 2023 (spotlight), EMNLP 2025 SAC highlight, and CCIR 2024 best paper candidate. He is supported by Scholar of Young Talent Promoting Project of CAST, and is the World's Top 2 % Scientists. He is a member of Chinese Information Processing Society of China, Youth Working Committee & Social Media Processing Committee & Information Retrieval Committee. He serves as AC of ACL, AAAI, WWW, EMNLP, NAACL, etc.

Recent work

Hybrid Gated Attention

Zekun Zhou, Ruobing Xie, Lanrui Wang, Weixuan Sun

This paper proposes Hybrid Gated Attention (HyGA), which combines three complementary multi-source gating strategies with low-rank decomposition and a learnable attention sink. HyGA reports consistent improvements over standard gated attention in training loss and downstream performance across backbones and compute budgets, reaching the Pareto frontier of effectiveness and efficiency. arXiv

Redesign Mixture-of-Experts Routers with Manifold Power Iteration

Songhao Wu, Ang Lv, Ruobing Xie, Yankai Lin

This paper proposes Manifold Power Iteration (MPI), which uses a "Power-then-Retract" update to align each MoE router row with the principal singular direction of its expert, building direct connections between router weights and their corresponding experts' capabilities. MPI provides convergence guarantees and shows gains in pretraining MoE models from 1B to 11B parameters. arXiv
